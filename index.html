With roots in the 1920s, Gauci Maistre & Gauci Maistre is a Valletta law firm advising and representing businesses, individuals and families in Malta and internationally. We combine an established legal tradition with practical advice and personal attention to each client’s affairs.

The Firm

Our heritage.
Your confidence.

Our origins lie in the legal practice established by Agostino Gauci-Maistre in the 1920s, before his appointment to the judiciary. Today, we carry that tradition forward through clear advice, careful preparation and a close understanding of our clients’ needs.

We advise on transactions and represent clients in disputes, providing direct access to the lawyer responsible for their matter and a consistent point of contact throughout.

Practice Areas

How we can help

  1. 01

    Litigation & Dispute Resolution

    Representation of businesses and individuals before the Maltese courts, tribunals and in arbitration, together with advice on the resolution of disputes before they reach court.

  2. 02

    Corporate & Commercial

    Shareholder arrangements, commercial contracts and transactions for businesses operating in or through Malta.

  3. 03

    Trademarks & Intellectual Property

    Registration, protection and enforcement of trademarks and other intellectual property rights, and the licensing and transfer of those rights.

  4. 04

    Property & Real Estate

    Acquisitions, sales, promise of sale agreements, leases and due diligence on property in Malta and Gozo.

  5. 05

    Residence & Citizenship

    Residence routes in Malta for non-EU individuals and families, including the Malta Permanent Residence Programme.
